Getting started

One of you creates your shared space with both your names and the day you got together, then sends your partner the invite code from the Us tab. They tap “Have an invite code? Join your partner” on their own phone, and from then on you both see everything either of you adds.

Forgot your passcode

When you create or join a couple, the app shows you a set of one-time recovery codes. Keep them somewhere safe — that is the only time they are shown. On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgotten your passcode?”, enter your email and one recovery code, and pick a new passcode. Each code works once.

Lost your codes too? Email us (below) and we’ll help.

Exporting your data

Us → Settings & account → Export everything gives you a file with every date, trip, event and caption.

Deleting your account

Delete account, on the same screen, erases the account, both logins, every entry and every photo. It is immediate, it applies to both of you, and it cannot be undone.

Notifications

The app can let you know when your partner adds something, and nudge you about upcoming plans and anniversaries. Turn any of these on or off under Us → Notifications.

Still stuck? Email support@its-just-between-us.com and a person will answer.
